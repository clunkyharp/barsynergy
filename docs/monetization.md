# Monetization

## MVP

No online payments in MVP.

Use manual application processing for:

- course applications;
- event applications;
- corporate training;
- supplier placement;
- vacancy placement;
- partner applications;
- certificate orders.

## Roadmap

1. Applications without online payment.
2. Manual paid education products.
3. Manual paid placements.
4. Partner packages.
5. Online payments after legal and accounting review.
6. Premium/subscription after regular audience appears.
7. B2B cabinets after stable MVP.

Do not create payment tables before the payment phase.
